# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

Define the functional and non-functional requirements of the Solar Monitoring system, an IoT platform designed to monitor solar energy generation and battery storage through a web-based dashboard accessible remotely.

The system acquires energy metrics from a small solar-powered installation, transmits telemetry through Wi-Fi, stores historical measurements, and provides real-time visualization capabilities.

---

## 1.2 Scope

The system shall:

- Monitor solar energy generation.
- Monitor battery charge and discharge behavior.
- Calculate electrical metrics such as voltage, current, and power.
- Transmit telemetry over Wi-Fi.
- Store historical records.
- Provide real-time visualization.
- Allow historical analysis.
- Correlate energy production with weather conditions.
- Operate using solar-generated energy as its primary power source.

---

## 1.3 Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| ESP32-C3 | RISC-V microcontroller with integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE |
| Telemetry | Remote transmission of measurements and operational data |
| SoC | State of Charge of a battery |
| IoT | Internet of Things |
| INA219 | I2C current and voltage monitoring sensor |
| Deep Sleep | Ultra-low power sleep mode supported by ESP32 |
| Firebase | Cloud platform used for data storage and synchronization |
| RSSI | Received Signal Strength Indicator |

# 3. Functional Requirements

## RF-001 - Solar Voltage Measurement

The system shall periodically measure the voltage generated by the solar panel.

## RF-002 - Solar Current Measurement

The system shall periodically measure the current provided by the solar panel.

## RF-003 - Solar Power Calculation

The system shall calculate instantaneous solar power using:

Power = Voltage × Current

## RF-004 - Battery Voltage Monitoring

The system shall measure battery voltage.

## RF-005 - Battery Current Monitoring

The system shall measure charge and discharge current.

## RF-006 - Battery State Estimation

The system shall estimate battery charge level.

## RF-007 - Telemetry Transmission

The device shall transmit telemetry through Wi-Fi.

## RF-008 - Data Persistence

The platform shall store telemetry records in a cloud database.

## RF-009 - Real-Time Monitoring

Users shall be able to view near real-time telemetry data.

## RF-010 - Historical Analysis

Users shall be able to access historical data records.

## RF-011 - System Status Visualization

The platform shall provide visibility into:

- ESP32 operational status.
- Wi-Fi connection status.
- Signal strength (RSSI).
- Last update timestamp.
- Battery health indicators.

## RF-012 - Weather Information

The platform shall display weather information relevant to energy production analysis.

## RF-013 - Measurement Timestamping

All telemetry records shall contain timestamps indicating measurement time.

## RF-014 - Production Trend Visualization

The dashboard shall present energy generation trends using graphical visualizations.

# 4. Non-Functional Requirements

## RNF-001 - Performance

The delay between acquisition and visualization shall not exceed:

30 seconds


under normal operating conditions.

## RNF-002 - Availability

The system shall operate continuously while sufficient energy is available.

Target:

24/7


operation under normal environmental conditions.

## RNF-003 - Scalability

The architecture shall support additional sensors without major redesign.

## RNF-004 - Security

Future versions shall support authentication mechanisms such as JWT.

Current MVP may expose public read-only telemetry.

## RNF-005 - Usability

The dashboard shall be fully responsive and usable on:

- Desktop devices.
- Tablets.
- Smartphones.

## RNF-006 - Energy Efficiency

The embedded system shall minimize energy consumption through power-saving strategies.

## RNF-007 - Maintainability

Core system components shall be modular and replaceable with minimal impact on the overall architecture.

## RNF-008 - Observability

The platform shall provide sufficient logging and diagnostic information for troubleshooting.

## RNF-009 - Reliability

Temporary network failures shall not require manual intervention to restore operation.

Wi-Fi 2.4GHz

## RST-003

The primary energy source shall be:

Solar Energy


stored in rechargeable 18650 batteries.

INA219 sensors

Firebase Realtime Database

# 8. Out of Scope (MVP)

The following features are excluded from the initial release:

- OTA firmware updates.
- Push notifications.
- Email alerts.
- Telegram integration.
- Multi-user management.
- Predictive analytics.
- Machine learning features.
- Home Assistant integration.
- Native mobile applications.

# 9. Success Criteria

The MVP shall be considered successful if:

- Solar telemetry is received successfully.
- Battery telemetry is received successfully.
- Data can be visualized remotely.
- Historical records are persisted.
- The system operates without external power.
- Dashboard access is possible from desktop and mobile devices.
